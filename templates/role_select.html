All domains share the same underlying EmotionLearnAI engine: real-time facial emotion inference (ONNX), 3D head pose attention tracking, multimodal cognitive state analysis, and personalized recommendation system. Emotion and attention signals are used as auxiliary behavioral indicators only — job-relevant assessment is always the primary evaluation factor.
